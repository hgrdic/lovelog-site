Chapters Diary

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Backslash d.o.o. ("Backslash," "we," "us"), a company registered in Zagreb, Croatia, collects, uses, and protects information when you use Chapters Diary (the "App"). Backslash d.o.o. is the data controller under the GDPR. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

Chapters Diary is a family diary: it is built for parents to record stories, photos, and milestones about their own children. It is not directed at children, is not intended for use by children on their own, and a child's profile in the App exists only as content their parent or guardian creates and controls.

Information we collect

We collect only what the App needs to work:

  • Account information — your email address and display name. Sign-in is by magic link or by Google or Apple sign-in; we do not store a password.
  • Family information — the names and dates of birth of the children you add, and your chosen theme. This is information a parent chooses to enter about their own child.
  • Your content — the stories, photos, milestones, growth measurements, weekly prompt answers, and letters you create. Photos may carry the date, and the image dimensions, read from the photo itself; if you turn on the optional location setting, a photo you add can also be labelled with a rough place name.
  • Push notification token — a device token used only to deliver the reminders and family notifications you have enabled.
  • Purchase information — your Chapters Premium subscription status (for example active, trialing, or expired). Apple processes the payment; we and our subscription provider, RevenueCat, only ever receive entitlement status, never your card details.
  • Usage analytics — anonymised, aggregated product analytics (which screens are opened, when the App is launched) through PostHog's EU instance, used to understand what to improve. No story, photo, milestone, prompt answer, or letter content is ever sent to analytics.
  • Diagnostic data — crash and error reports, configured to capture crash context only, with personal information and session capture turned off.

What we don't do

  • We don't read your stories, prompt answers, or letters.
  • We don't build advertising profiles or show ads.
  • We don't sell your data, or your child's information, to anyone.
  • We don't use your content to train AI models.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't knowingly collect information directly from a child — every entry is made by the parent or co-parent account.

Letters

Letters you seal, including Letters to the Future written for a child to read at a chosen age, are encrypted before they are stored and stay closed until the reveal date you choose. The key is issued to your signed-in devices by our backend and held only in memory while the App is running, never written to disk. This protects letter contents at rest and keeps a sealed letter unreadable before its date; it is not a zero-knowledge system, so treat it as strong protection rather than a guarantee that no one but you could ever technically access the ciphertext.

How your data is stored

Your data is stored on Cloudflare infrastructure in the European Union: a D1 database for account, family, and content data, and R2 object storage for photos. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is scoped to your family's relationship, so no one outside it can see your content.

Who we share data with

We share data only with the service providers that help us run the App, each acting on our behalf:

  • Cloudflare — database, file storage, and backend hosting (EU region).
  • Apple / RevenueCat — subscription purchases and status. Apple processes your payment through the App Store; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user identifier to sync your subscription. Neither we nor RevenueCat see your card details.
  • Resend — transactional email only: your magic-link sign-in, invite links to a co-parent, and, if you request it, a keepsake export sent to your own email address.
  • PostHog (EU instance) — anonymised product analytics as described above.
  • Google / Apple — if you choose Google or Apple sign-in, to authenticate you. We receive your email address and a stable identifier, nothing else from your account.

We do not otherwise share your personal data, and we do not sell it.

Your co-parent

Chapters is a shared space for a family. Stories, photos, milestones, growth entries, and revealed letters that any parent in the family adds are visible to the other parent in the same family. An invite is sent to an email address you enter, and only becomes a link between two accounts once the other person accepts it.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from the Account screen in the App. Deletion is immediate and irreversible, and removes your family's account, login, children's information, and content from our systems, including the photos stored for that family. Apple may retain billing records as required by law, which is outside our control.

Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and your child's information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can edit your content directly in the App, export a keepsake copy from the App, and delete your account from the Account screen, or contact us at the email below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority, in Croatia the Agencija za zaštitu osobnih podataka (AZOP).

Children's privacy

The App itself is not directed at children under 16 and is not designed for a child to use on their own — a child's name, date of birth, photos, and milestones are entered and managed by their parent or guardian, the account holder. We do not knowingly allow a child to create or control their own account, and we do not knowingly collect personal data directly from a child. If you believe a child has provided us information directly, contact us at the email below and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make a material change, we'll update the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we'll notify you by email.
