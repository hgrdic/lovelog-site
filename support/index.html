Pairing with your partner

From the home screen, send an invite to your partner's email address, or share the pairing link or code directly. Your two accounts are linked once they accept. Each person can be in one relationship at a time.

Signing in

LoveLog uses a magic link sent to your email, or Google or Apple sign-in. There is no password. If a magic link doesn't arrive, check spam, make sure the address is spelled the way you registered it, and request a new one, older links expire.

Back up or move your data

Open Account and use the backup export to save a copy of your memories, photos, answers, and events. Signing in on a new device restores your shared space from the account, so there is nothing to transfer by hand.

Managing Premium

LoveLog Premium is billed by Apple. To view, change, or cancel it, open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. One subscription covers both partners. For refund requests use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete your account

Open Account and choose to delete your account. This is immediate and irreversible: it removes your login and personal data, and shared content is removed once both accounts are deleted.

Turn off notifications

Turn off reminders in the App, or manage LoveLog's notification permission from your device's Settings → Notifications.
