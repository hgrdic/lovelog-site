A private journal for the two of you

LoveLog is a shared space for couples to keep memories, letters, and the dates that matter, together on one timeline.

Private to the two of you · No ads · Export or delete anytime

One place for your relationship

Not a social network. A quiet, shared notebook only the two of you can open.

A shared timeline

Save memories with photos, a date, and a place. Both of you write to the same timeline, so the story fills in from both sides.

A question every day

A new prompt each day. You both answer in private, then each answer opens once you have both replied.

Letters that open later

Write a letter to your partner, seal it, and pick the date it unlocks. It stays encrypted and closed until that day arrives.

Dates that don't slip

A shared calendar for anniversaries, birthdays, and trips, a running count of your time together, and a reminder before the day.

Your year, retold

Our Year gathers your milestones, photos, and streaks into a recap you can look back on together, and export as a keepsake.

Private by design

Your content is visible only to the two of you. No ads, no data sold, no content used to train AI. Export or delete everything whenever you want.
